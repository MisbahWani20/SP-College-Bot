About Cluster University Of Srinagar
What is a Cluster University Of Srinagar?
Most of the State Universities are overburdened with hundreds of affiliated colleges and as a step to decongest these Universities, Government has decided to establish Cluster Universities in the States. These Cluster Universities shall be created from the existing four to five Colleges that are located in close vicinity. The infrastructure development of the Cluster Universities is funded through the Rashtriya Uchchattar Shiksha Abhiyan (RUSA) scheme of the Ministry of Human Resources and Development (MHRD).
What are the constituent colleges of the Cluster University of Srinagar?
The Cluster University of Srinagar (CUS) has been created by pooling the resources of five existing colleges of Amar Singh College, Sri Pratap College, Govt. College for Women, M. A. Road, Govt. Degree College, Bemina and Govt. College of Education. These five constituent colleges of the Cluster University have adequate academic, physical and technical infrastructural facilities and the mission of CUS would be to lead these colleges to the advanced schools of learning.
Will Cluster University of Srinagar be associated with University of Kashmir ?
Cluster University of Srinagar shall be an independent University and shall conduct examinations and award degrees following its own rules and  regulations. It shall have its own regulatory bodies with Hon’ble Chief Minster as Chancellor of the Cluster University.
Is Cluster University of Srinagar a State or a Central University ?
Cluster University of Srinagar is a State University and has been established under “The Srinagar and Jammu Cluster Universities Act, 2016″ passed by the Jammu and Kashmir State Legislature on July 8, 2016. Two Cluster Universities in the State are similar to University of Kashmir/Jammu.
Have you launched some programmes this session?
We have launched eight 5-year Integrated programmes in this first session of the University and next session we shall be offering many more programmes.
How are these programmes different from other programmes?
It needs to be clarified first that all the existing programmes in the five constituent colleges shall continue as earlier. The eight Integrated programmes are on the top of these existing courses. The importance of the Integrated programmes is that a student shall be able to choose his specialized discipline at an early stage and can remain focused in that subject. Most of the 5-year programmes shall have the exit option after completion of 3-years with B. A (B. Sc) honors degrees. After successfully completing 5-years, candidates shall be awarded Masters degrees. The advantage of the 5-year Integrated programme is that a candidate after successfully completing 3-years is assured of admission in the PG part of the programme. It is important to mention that curriculum of these courses shall be as per UGC or AICTE regulations, depending upon the course.
What shall be the criteria for admission of the students in the Cluster University Of Srinagar?
For all under-graduate programmes, the admission shall be purely based on the marks obtained in the 12th standard. For integrated programmes, 50% weightage shall be given to the combined marks of 10th and 12th standards and 50% shall be reserved for the general aptitude test. The test shall have three sections of general English, reasoning and basic mathematics.
What shall be the admission fee structure?
Admission fee structure shall be same as that of last year. As a matter of fact, for some courses, the fee shall be reduced as it was found to be unfair.
Who shall award the degrees?
All the degress, whether it is under-graduate or post-graduate, shall be awarded by the Cluster University of Srinagar, henceforth. However, it is important to add that those students of the five constituent colleges, who are in the second or third year of their studies, shall continue to remain affiliated with the University of Kashmir and shall be awarded degrees by this University.

Instructions with regard to term end question paper for under graduate and five year integrated programmes session 2017
Term End Examination Scheme for theory part
Total Units 4 Total marks per unit 15 Total marks: 60
1-2-4-8 Model for distribution of marks
For each unit, 1 mark shall be awarded for attendance
(1 x 4= 4 marks)
For each unit, 2 marks shall be awarded for two objective type questions
(2 x 4= 8 marks)
For each unit, 4 marks shall be awarded for Applied Questions
(4 x 4= 16 marks)
For each unit, 8 marks shall be awarded for Descriptive Questions
(4 x 8 = 32 marks)
As per the above pattern the question paper setter shall be set for 56 Marks only and 04 marks shall be awarded for attendance.
The Question Paper of the Term End Examination shall have questions of three types:
1.  6 Long Answer Type Questions out of which candidate will be asked to attempt 4.
2.  6 Problem Solving Type Questions out of which candidates will be asked to attempt 4.
3.  8 Objective Type Questions with suggested options of answers. (All Compulsory)
For the first two sections of the Question Paper (containing Long and Problem-Solving Type Questions) four questions shall
be based on four different units of the syllabus. The additional two questions in the two sections shall be set in such a
manner so that all the 4 units of syllabus are covered. That is if the additional two questions in Long Answer Question Section
are from Units I & II, the additional two questions in the Problem-Solving Question Section shall be from Unit IIIrd and IVth.

Instructions for Paper Setters with regard to Term End Examination question paper for four year Degree Programme in Engineering and Technology (session 2017)
Total Units:
Four
Marks per Unit:
25
Total Marks:
100
Question paper shall have following three Sections containing questions of three types
Section - A
06 long answer type questions out of which candidate shall be asked to attempt four only. 14 marks shall be awarded to each question.
(14x4 = 56 marks)
Section - B
06 problem solving type questions out of which candidate shall be asked to attempt four only. 07 marks shall be awarded for each question.
(7X4 = 28 marks)
Section - C
08 objective type questions selecting two questions from each Unit. Two marks shall be awarded for each objective type question.
(2X8 = 16 marks)
Note:
For the first two sections of the question paper (containing long and problem solving type questions) 04 questions shall be based on four different units of the syllabus. The additional two questions in the two sections shall be set in such a manner so that all the four units of syllabus are covered. If the additional two questions in long answer type question section are from units I and II the additional two questions in the problem solving question section shall be from Unit III and IV.

About CU Srinagar
The Cluster University of Srinagar (CUS) has been created by pooling the resources of five existing colleges of Amar Singh College,
Sri Pratap College, Govt. College for Women, M. A. Road, Govt. Degree College, Bemina and Govt. College of Education.
These five constituent colleges of the Cluster University have adequate academic, physical and technical infrastructural
facilities and
more..
The Concept
The Cluster University Of Srinagar concept, first of its kind in India introduced by the Jammu and Kashmir Government in the year 2016. It is expected to give an impetus to academics and research in the state.

Academic Council
Cluster University of Srinagar
As per the University statutes, The Academic Council may appoint a Standing Committee to:
grant equivalence to the courses and examinations of other Universities with the corresponding courses and examinations.
to prescribe qualifications for admission to various programmes, to determine the intake of students in each programme and other
matters related to the admission of students and their examinations;.
to consider the minutes of Board of studies, Board of Research studies and to make suitable recommendations to the Academic council;
to approve the Academic Calendar of the University; and
to consider any other Academic matter referred to it by the Vice-Chancellor as may deem fit for approval/endorsement on behalf of the Academic Council.

About CU Srinagar
The Cluster University of Srinagar (CUS) has been created by pooling the resources of five existing colleges of Amar Singh College,
Sri Pratap College, Govt. College for Women, M. A. Road, Govt. Degree College, Bemina and Govt. College of Education.
These five constituent colleges of the Cluster University have adequate academic, physical and technical infrastructural

The Concept
The Cluster University Of Srinagar concept, first of its kind in India introduced by the Jammu and Kashmir Government in the year 2016. It is expected to give an impetus to academics and research in the state.
